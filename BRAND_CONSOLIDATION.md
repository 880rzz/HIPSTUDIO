# HIPStudio master-brand consolidation

Decision date: 2026-09-08
Status: approved strategic direction; review build only until production gates are closed.

## Decision

HIPStudio is the master brand and `https://www.hipstudio.hu/` is the target master domain for the unified platform.

HIPStudio has a source-backed founding date of 2006-02-27. The brand's long operating history and verified service catalogue provide the strongest continuity and trust base for the consolidated platform.

## Why the three service businesses are being integrated

This is a brand and service integration, not an assertion of a legal corporate merger unless the legal structure is separately documented.

The customer rationale is straightforward: clients increasingly need connected solutions rather than isolated suppliers. Creative production, business operations, finance/control support, process improvement, AI preparation and corporate experiences often touch the same business objective. Bringing the capabilities into one HIPStudio customer journey lets a client coordinate fewer suppliers and access a broader combination of expertise through one partner relationship.

The integration should therefore be explained as an expansion of capability built on HIPStudio's existing professional history — not as the replacement of HIPStudio by a new, unknown umbrella brand.

## Brand architecture

- **HIPStudio** — master brand and master domain.
- **HIPStudio Business** — Business / Operations pillar; HelloÜzlet is treated as a legacy/source brand during migration, not as the master brand.
- **HIPStudio Creative** — the established creative/content capability and direct continuation of the historic HIPStudio service base.
- **Flúgos by HIPStudio** — Experience / Events pillar; Flúgos retains its specialist identity and historical story while becoming part of the common customer journey.

No Schema.org `parentOrganization`, `subOrganization`, legal ownership, trademark ownership or contracting-party relationship should be asserted merely from this brand architecture. Those claims require separate legal evidence.

## Core narrative

A HIPStudio 2006 óta épít kommunikációs és kreatív megoldásokat. Az elmúlt két évtizedben az ügyfelek igényei megváltoztak: ma egy videó, egy kampány vagy egy céges esemény gyakran ugyanahhoz a problémához kapcsolódik, mint a működés, a vezetői információ, a folyamatok vagy az automatizáció.

Ezért a HIPStudio szolgáltatási rendszere kibővül. A kreatív és kommunikációs tapasztalat mellé üzleti működési, pénzügyi-kontrolling, folyamatfejlesztési és AI-előkészítési kompetenciák, valamint a Flúgos vállalati élménytervezése kapcsolódik.

Nem három külön szolgáltatót teszünk egymás mellé. Egy olyan partneri rendszert építünk, amelyben az ügyfél kevesebb szereplőt koordinál, miközben több összefüggő üzleti problémára kap megoldást.

**Egy HIPStudio. Három szakmai pillér. Több lehetőség az ügyfélnek.**

### EN

**20 years of experience. Now as a more complete business partner.**

HIPStudio has been building communications and creative solutions since 2006. Client needs have evolved: today a video, campaign or corporate event often connects to the same underlying challenges as operations, management information, process design or automation.

HIPStudio is therefore expanding its service system. Business operations, finance and control, process improvement and AI-preparation capabilities are being added to the established creative and communications base, together with Flúgos corporate experience design.

This is not three separate suppliers placed next to each other. It is one partner model designed to reduce coordination and solve more connected business needs through one customer journey.

**One HIPStudio. Three specialist pillars. More capability for the client.**

### DE

**20 Jahre Erfahrung. Jetzt als umfassenderer Business-Partner.**

HIPStudio entwickelt seit 2006 Kommunikations- und Kreativlösungen. Die Anforderungen der Kunden haben sich weiterentwickelt: Ein Video, eine Kampagne oder ein Firmenevent hängt heute häufig mit denselben grundlegenden Themen zusammen wie Betrieb, Managementinformationen, Prozesse oder Automatisierung.

Deshalb erweitert HIPStudio sein Leistungssystem. Zu der etablierten Kreativ- und Kommunikationsbasis kommen Business Operations, Finance & Control, Prozessverbesserung und KI-Vorbereitung sowie das Corporate Experience Design von Flúgos hinzu.

Es werden nicht drei getrennte Anbieter nebeneinandergestellt. Es entsteht ein gemeinsames Partnermodell, das Koordinationsaufwand reduziert und mehr zusammenhängende geschäftliche Anforderungen in einer Customer Journey abdeckt.

**Ein HIPStudio. Drei Fachsäulen. Mehr Möglichkeiten für den Kunden.**

## Migration implications

1. Canonical production domain becomes `hipstudio.hu`.
2. `hellouzlet.hu` becomes a migration/redirect asset for Business-related legacy traffic rather than the umbrella domain.
3. `flugos.hu` remains a specialist/heritage entry point and should ultimately redirect or route to the corresponding HIPStudio Experience pages according to the approved URL migration matrix.
4. Existing HIPStudio SEO equity and historical URLs must be preserved through explicit 301 mappings.
5. Public copy should distinguish brand/service integration from any legal merger unless legal evidence is approved.
6. The 2006 founding date and twenty-year continuity may be used where the provenance record remains attached to the claim.
