# Business pillar source and claim governance

Audit date: 2026-09-08

Status: approved strategic brief translated into implementation guardrails. This is not evidence of a legal entity, regulated-service authorization or production contracting structure.

## Approved strategic scope

The unified platform may model the Business / Operations pillar around the following service directions supplied in the approved project brief:

- financial administration;
- payroll and HR administration support;
- back-office / executive operations support;
- management reporting and controlling;
- process improvement and operating-system design;
- grant-related support as acquisition/cross-sell where separately verified;
- AI readiness audit;
- implementation sprint;
- managed AI operations direction;
- fractional CFO / finance-control direction where delivery scope and responsible professional are separately approved.

## Approved target customer logic

Primary target segment for positioning and information architecture:

- Hungarian SMEs, typically 10–100 employees;
- owner or managing director still carries substantial operational load;
- no complete internal CFO/COO/specialist capacity across all functions;
- fragmented administration and reporting;
- need for greater management visibility and operating discipline;
- interest in automation/AI without a controlled implementation model.

This is a go-to-market definition, not a claim about current client composition.

May describe readiness assessment, use-case/process mapping, data/risk checkpoints and implementation planning. No AI certification, guaranteed savings, legal-compliance guarantee or autonomous decision-making claim may be made.

## Mandatory production gates

Before any regulated or professional service is presented as actively contractable, verify as applicable:

- exact contracting legal entity;
- responsible professional/provider;
- whether the activity is performed directly, by partner or by subcontractor;
- professional/regulatory requirements;
- scope/exclusions;
- pricing or quotation rules;
- data-controller/processor roles;
- retention and processor/subprocessor information;
- insurance/certification claims if any;
- territorial availability.

## Wording constraints

Until the above gates are resolved:

Use:

- „támogatás” / “support”;
- „koordináció” where delivery responsibility is not verified;
- „szolgáltatási irány” / intended service direction;
- „vezetői riporting és kontroll”;
- „AI readiness / implementációs előkészítés”.

Avoid unsupported statements such as:

- „mi könyveljük a cégedet” unless the contracting/accounting provider is verified;
- guaranteed tax, funding, savings or compliance outcomes;
- certified AI/compliance statements;
- invented team size, client count or market leadership;
- unverified cross-brand ownership/legal relationships.
